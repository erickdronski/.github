# Code of conduct

## Standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather than the person. Welcome good-faith questions and differences in experience. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use project spaces for spam or unrelated promotion.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces maintained by Erick Dronski.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious conduct concerns should be raised privately through the contact routes on Erick's GitHub profile rather than debated publicly.
